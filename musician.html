Story

From words in a diary to stories in music.

I started writing long before I knew where these songs would take me. I wrote, just to be heard, to be understood and just to be felt being existed. What began as scattered lyrics, melodies and thoughts gradually became a way of expressing what I felt, imagined and couldn't always say out loud.

And at this point, songwriting is more than something I do. It is the way I tell stories. With more than a 100+ original compositions written across different phases of my life, It feel so good to share the feeling of love, longing, connection, distance, memories and the little moments that stay with us for the life time.
